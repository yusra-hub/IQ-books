Empowering Students

IQ Book is a gamified learning and competitive education platform in
Pakistan designed to help students improve their academic performance
through quizzes, MCQs, and interactive challenges.

Who We Are

We are an education-focused platform built for students who want to:


   Improve their MCQ solving skills

   Prepare for competitive exams like MDCAT & ECAT

   Build consistency and discipline in learning

   Compete in a fair, performance-based environment


IQ Book is not just another study app it is a performance-driven learning ecosystem.

Our Mission

Our mission is to transform traditional education in
Pakistan by introducing:

   Gamified learning experiences

   Competitive quiz-based challenges

   Performance tracking systems

   Merit-based scholarship opportunities


We believe that hardworking and consistent students
deserve recognition and opportunities based on their
performance, not luck.
