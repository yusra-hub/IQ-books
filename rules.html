IQ Book Scholarship: Mukammal Guide

Seekho, Khelo aur Jeeto! No Fees, No Luck, Sirf Merit.

Humara System Kaise Kaam Karta Hai?

IQ Book ka maqsad Pakistan ke har student ko muft taleem aur mali madad (scholarship) dena hai. Hum app mein Ads dikhate hain taake funds ikatha kar saken, aur wahi funds hum apne Top Merit Students ko scholarship ki surat mein dete hain. Aap ads dekhte hain, aur seekhte hain, aur hum aapki parhai mein invest karte hain!

Scholarship ka Safar (Step-by-Step)

1. Practice aur Learning (Hamesha Jari)

Scholarship open hone se pehle Brain Test aur Rapid Fire mein practice karein. Mushkil sawal hal karne ke liye video ads dekhein aur Power-Ups (Skip, Time Booster) hasil karein.

2. 10 Din ka Event (Asli Muqabla)

Scholarship machine mein sirf 2 se 3 dafa open hoti hai. In dino mein aapko ye targets poore karne honge:

  • Accuracy: Har challenge mein +75% score lazmi hai.
  • 1v1 Challenge: Head-to-head muqablon mein aapka Win Rate 50% hona chahiye.
  • Consistency: In 10 dino mein active reh kar zyada se zyada Brain Test matches khelein.

3. Apply Kaise Karein? (The Process)

Aap tab tak apply nahi kar sakte jab tak aap eligibility target complete na karein.

Targets sirf apply karne ki eligibility dete hain, final selection sirf top Brain scorers ka hota hai.

  • App ke andar hi application form fill karein.
  • Form submit karte hi aapka status "Under Review" ho jaye ga.

4. Team Rabta aur Tasdeeq (Verification)

Application submit hone ke baad hamari Support Team aapki gameplay aur details check kare gi.

  • Team Contact: Hamari team aap se email ya phone par rabta kar sakti hai.
  • Documents: Aap se aapke School/College/University ka card ya result sheet mangi jaye gi taake tasdeeq ho sakay ke aap waqai ek talib-e-ilm (student) hain.
  • Final Status: Verification ke baad aapka status "Verified Candidate" ho jaye ga aur final selection merit par hogi.

Fair Play Code (Rules)

  • Bots & Cheating: Kisi bhi qism ki cheating par account foran ban ho jaye ga.
  • Multiple Accounts: Ek banda sirf ek hi account use kar sakta hai.
  • 50% Accuracy Rule: 1v1 challenge mein agar aapki accuracy 50% se kam hui, to aap disqualified ho jayein ge (bhale hi aap jeet jayein). Humein sirf serious students chahiye!

Security measures include:

  • Device verification
  • Account validation
  • Activity monitoring

Active rehne, smart khelne aur honestly compete karne wale users ke jeetne ke chances sabse zyada hain.

News, Updates aur merit awards miss na karne ke liye hamara WhatsApp channel follow karein.
