How IQ Book
Scholarship System
Works

IQ Book is a merit-based gamified learning platform in Pakistan where
students improve their academic performance through quizzes, MCQs, and
competitive challenges to become eligible for scholarship opportunities.
Unlike traditional systems, IQ Book does not rely on luck. Instead, it
focuses on performance, consistency, and skill improvement.





Daily Practice & Skill Building

IQ Book is a gamified learning platform for students in Pakistan designed to improve
academic performance through structured practice and competition.

Participate in Scholarship Events

IQ Book organizes 10-day scholarship events (2–3 times per month)
where students compete in real-time challenges.


During these events, students must:

   Maintain high accuracy (75% or more)

   Compete in 1v1 quiz battles

   Stay consistent throughout the event period

Performance during these events is critical for eligibility.

Performance Evaluation

Students are evaluated based on:


   Quiz accuracy

   Daily consistency

   Win rate in battles

   Overall leaderboard ranking


Only students who meet the required performance standards
become eligible for scholarship consideration.

Application Submission


Once eligibility criteria are met:


   Application status becomes “Under Review”

   Students can apply through the platform

   Basic student information is verified

Verification Process

After submission, the support team reviews each application:


   Student identity verification

   Academic confirmation (school/college ID or result sheet)

   Contact via email or phone if needed

Once verified, status is updated to “Verified Candidate”.

Merit-Based Selection

Final selection is based on:


Only top-performing students are awarded scholarship opportunities.

Important Note


   Scholarships are not guaranteed

   Selection is based purely on merit and performance

   IQ Book ensures fairness through strict monitoring and
anti-cheating systems

Why This System Exists


IQ Book aims to:

   Improve student learning habits

   Encourage competition among students

   Reward consistent performance

   Promote merit-based education in Pakistan
